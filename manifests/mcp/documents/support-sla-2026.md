This document replaces support-sla-2024 from 1 July 2026.

First response times, around the clock for P1 and in business hours otherwise:
- P1, the service is down for the customer: 1 hour.
- P2, a feature the customer depends on is broken: 4 business hours.
- P3, everything else: 2 business days.

A missed P1 response earns the customer a credit of 5% of that month's fee, a missed P2 response 2%.
