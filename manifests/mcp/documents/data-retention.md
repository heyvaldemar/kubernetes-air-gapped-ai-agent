id: data-retention
title: Customer data retention
audience: all
updated: 2026-05-20
---
Customer data is kept for 90 days after a contract ends, then deleted. Backups are kept for 35 days and expire on their own.

A customer may ask for their data to be deleted earlier. We confirm the request in writing and complete it within 30 days, backups included.

We never keep customer data for training or analytics after deletion.
