id: refund-policy
title: Refunds and service credits
audience: all
updated: 2026-09-01
---
Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, an annual plan is refunded pro rata for the whole months left, and only when the customer leaves because of a service failure we have confirmed.

Monthly plans are not refunded. A customer who cancels keeps the service to the end of the month already paid.

Service credits for missed response times are set by the support SLA, not by this policy, and are applied to the next invoice, never paid out.
