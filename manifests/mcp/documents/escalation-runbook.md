---
id: escalation-runbook
title: Escalating a P1 incident
audience: all
updated: 2026-08-12
---
1. Open the incident in the incident tracker and set the severity to P1.
2. Page the on-call engineer from the tracker. Do not phone them directly.
3. Post the incident link in the incident channel within 10 minutes of the customer's report.
4. Send the customer a first update within 30 minutes, then every hour until the service is restored.
5. The support lead decides when the incident is closed and owns the customer summary.
