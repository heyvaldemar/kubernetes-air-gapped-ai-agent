List prices per seat per month: Team 12 USD, Business 28 USD, Enterprise by quote.

An account manager may discount up to 15% on their own authority. From 15% to 25% the regional sales director approves; above 25% the VP of sales approves. Multi-year contracts may add 5% on top of the approved discount.
